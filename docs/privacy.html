PRIVACY POLICY

BITTY BOX PROTOCOL · LAST UPDATED: SEPTEMBER 2026
ZERO-SERVER PAYLOAD RETENTION BY DESIGN

BittyBox is built on an absolute zero-knowledge privacy foundation. When you compile an application capsule, the data is embedded solely into the URL hash fragment. Our servers never log, store, or view the code inside your links.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Due to the mathematical and cryptographic structure of URL hash fragments (#...):

  • No Capsule Storage: We do not store, index, or inspect the HTML, JavaScript, CSS, or assets inside generated capsules.
  • No Key Interception: Client-side encryption keys and passwords never leave your browser runtime.
  • No Third-Party Ad Trackers: We do not embed ad network trackers, social analytics pixels, or sell user telemetry.

2. Information We Collect for Accounts & Service Delivery

When you opt into optional developer or user services (such as Magic Link authentication or notification delivery):

  • Email Address: Used solely to send cryptographic magic login links and check-in notification alerts.
  • Heartbeat Metadata: Timestamps of check-in pings required to maintain switch status and countdown schedules.
  • Diagnostic Logs: Transient server operational logs containing IP addresses and request durations, automatically rotated and purged.

3. Local Storage & Client Data Control

BittyBox utilizes your browser's local storage (localStorage / sessionStorage) exclusively to store your workspace theme, draft sessions, walkthrough status, and local box history for your convenience. You can purge this data at any time through your browser settings.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information. Third-party infrastructure providers (such as transactional email providers for heartbeat alerts) process minimal operational data strictly as necessary to execute requested notifications.

5. Security Practices

All network communications use modern TLS/HTTPS encryption in transit. Cryptographic primitives and URL-native hashes ensure that secret payloads remain protected even against network intermediaries.

6. Contact Regarding Privacy

If you have questions about privacy or wish to request the deletion of your account records, please contact our team at support@bittybox.org.
